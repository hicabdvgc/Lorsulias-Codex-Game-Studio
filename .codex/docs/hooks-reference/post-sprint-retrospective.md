# Post-sprint retrospective (manual workflow)

Invoke `$retrospective sprint-N` or `$retrospective milestone-name` for the exact
period. Any available qualified executor may analyze supplied evidence. Story files
own current status; historical conclusions need period-specific records. Comparable
observations support trends; unknown effort, causes and team reflections remain
explicit. Existing authorization covers the requested record and scoped updates.

New records use `production/retrospectives/retro-[scope]-[date].md`; existing legacy
records may be updated when identified. Preserve earlier records and verify saves.
Actions, owners and deadlines reflect supplied agreements. No automatic lifecycle
hook is registered. Sprint planning may consume the result when requested; milestone
achievement and gate orchestration use the separate installed milestone-review and gate-check workflows.
