# Godot audio

Library baseline: **4.7.2**. Documentation checked **2026-10-08**.

## Capabilities and choices

Audio buses route streams through effects to the master mix. Separate buses by an actual mixing/control need. Consider positional versus non-positional playback, polyphony and clipping. Do not equate a documented audio feature with a measured latency or an accessibility result.

## Application and verification

Trace a UI confirmation and positional world cue through their player/bus routes. Verify settings persistence, simultaneous playback, audible feedback and any visual/caption alternative required by ACCESSIBILITY. Record actual listening or capture evidence.
